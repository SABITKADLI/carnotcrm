## Design Context

### Users
Fabric sourcing company administrators, production coordinators and external tailors. They purchase and sell fabric, manufacture garments, review cutting plans, track progress, bill customers and publish finished goods.

### Brand Personality
Precise, composed, crafted. The user chose a refined fashion-studio style.

### Aesthetic Direction
Light studio workspace with warm paper surfaces, deep ink typography, restrained rust accents, fine rules and generous page margins. Distinctive display headings paired with legible operational text. Data tables and garment workflow take priority over decorative imagery.

### Design Principles
- Trace each garment from material to finished sale.
- Expose status and next action without clutter.
- Keep phone interactions practical for tailors.
- Use quiet editorial hierarchy and visible, accessible controls.
- Show real persisted operational data; label sample data and estimates honestly.
